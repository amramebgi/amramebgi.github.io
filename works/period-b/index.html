Period B

2004 - 2010 — Later works including digital prints and new explorations. 155 works.

Interpretations of the Hebrew texts are ongoing, but current analyses have identified two recurring themes:

  1. Expressions of delusional paranoia, including feelings of being targeted by conspiracies or having misguided beliefs about others' characteristics.
  2. Disappointment felt due to a lack of support during difficult times.

Preserving the artistic legacy of master etcher Amram Ebgi for future generations.
