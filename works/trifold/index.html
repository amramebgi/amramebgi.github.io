Trifold Works

2010 — Large-scale trifold compositions. 81 works.

The Hebrew text in this series delves into challenging themes of paranoia and imagined-victimization. Amram occasionally tries to overcome his mental struggles by promoting full personal responsibility. Yet, these instances are mixed with further expressions of paranoia, particularly his persistent belief that imaginary malevolent forces are thwarting his goals.

The Hebrew text also contains poetic odes resembling prayers, as well as excerpts from the Bible.

The series also includes collages of photographic memorabilia from the happier times of Period A. Furthermore, several pieces feature charming drawings created with color markers and watercolors, techniques he often used in his later works.

Please be aware that the sequence of the Trifolds is random and was never specified by the artist. The numbers are solely used for organization and identification purposes and hold no additional significance.

Preserving the artistic legacy of master etcher Amram Ebgi for future generations.
