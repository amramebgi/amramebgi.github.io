Early Works

Before 1980 — Formative explorations in abstraction and figurative art. 145 works.

Many of the pieces displayed seem to be from Ebgi's student days, especially the oil landscapes and boat scenes. The intaglio works appear to have been created later. Please be aware that the dating of these works is still ongoing.

Preserving the artistic legacy of master etcher Amram Ebgi for future generations.
